Le Cameron in focus

Transmission timeline

The record makes more sense when the title, translations, and publishers are seen as a sequence.

1349-53Boccaccio writes the Italian Decameron.
1411-14Premierfait translates from a Latin intermediary for Jean de Berry.
1485Verard prints and reworks the text for a new reading public.
1534The corpus edition circulates as Le Cameron.
1545Le Macon translates directly from Italian and supersedes the older line.
Research angle

Why the three records belong together

Le Cameron

Shows how a translation becomes a publisher-shaped print object, and how women in the frame function as speakers and judges.

Defensione delle donne

Contrasts print circulation with manuscript survival, while keeping the pro-woman polemical tradition visible.

Recueil des dames illustres

Expands the corpus toward catalogues of exemplary women, shifting attention to packaging, attribution, and later editions.
